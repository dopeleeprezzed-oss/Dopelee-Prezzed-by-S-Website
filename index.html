Every keepsake tells a story. But too often, those stories get lost, tucked away in old phones, forgotten hard drives, or voicemails you replay until they fade.

The Legacy Archive Upgrade transforms this reality. We take your most meaningful moments and make them part of the gift itself. With a custom QR code seamlessly integrated into your keepsake, your memories aren't hidden anymore. They're unlocked, beautifully presented, and preserved for the next generation.

Legacy Showcase • Starting at $100

For stories too rich to keep in a folder. A private, custom-designed page that showcases your memories with images, video embeds, and text, beautifully laid out like a digital gallery. Expand any section for $45 each.
